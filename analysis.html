Analysis

What they do, why it works, and what is worth taking.

Do not merge this with Shelby Sapp. Two different companies share the name “She Sells”. Shelby Sapp runs She Sells Remote in the US — the $7,000 closer mentorship that is the textbook example in this swipe file. This is an Australian business fronted by Brooke. Different country, different offer, different Facebook Page, different funnel. Any analysis or copy bank that blends them is wrong.

Why they matter to us

This is the closest ICP overlap in the entire file: career-driven women who want out of a job, sold a remote sales skill and a promise of time freedom. They are running 143 active ads against that audience, so they are a live competitor for the same attention, not a curiosity.

The copy order is the lesson

Their orderWhat it does
1. The feelingExhausted, guilty, stuck, stressed about bills
2. The recognition“Most women are never shown a way out”
3. The personBrooke, a teacher who left
4. The mechanismA sales skill that can be learned
5. The numberIncome, last

The money arrives after the identity has been sold. Our copy tends to lead with the outcome figure, which asks the reader to believe a number before they believe the story.

Worth taking

Kill the objection in the ad

“No MLM nonsense, no degree needed” runs in paid, before the click. Cheaper than handling it on the page, and it filters who arrives.

Try a quiz as the opt-in

Same fields, less felt commitment, and it qualifies while it collects. A quiz feels like self-discovery rather than a form.

Sell the identity before the income

Five beats before a number appears. Ours is faster to the money and may be asking for belief too early.

In-person events as a channel

Their Perth live-event ad is one of their best performers. Live events are a channel we have not touched at all.

Read carefully

Who actually owns this business is unconfirmed. Brooke fronts the VSL and describes herself as a former teacher, but that does not establish that she owns the company. The Facebook Page carries the brand name, not a person's.

No price is claimed because none appears in the captured funnel. Figures spoken in the VSL are described as outcomes, not as the cost of the program, and should not be repeated as pricing.
